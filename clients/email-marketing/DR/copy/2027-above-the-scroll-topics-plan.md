## 1. The one goal for 2027

**Get readers to click.** People already open and read Above the Scroll (2025: 17–27% opens, 58–84% read rate). Almost nobody clicks (about 0.5–0.8% on General, often one click on Franchise). Every decision below is made to close that gap.

| Measure | 2025 level | 2027 target | Why this target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Click rate (main number)** | 0.5–0.8% | **2.0% by June, 2.5% by December** | About back to the 2023 baseline (1.9%), then a little past it |
| Booked consults from the email | Not tracked | 1 per quarter, tracked by UTM | Proves the newsletter feeds sales |
| Open rate (health check only) | 17–27% | Hold at 20%+ | We are not chasing opens |
| Unsubscribe rate | 0.4–1.0% | 0.46% or lower | The existing goal |
| Time on site from email | ~18 sec | 45 sec+ | Send people to one useful page, not the homepage |

## 2. What we learned, and the rule each lesson sets

| What happened (2019–2025) | 2027 rule |
|---|---|
| SEO, Google and AI topics got the most clicks every time | At least 7 of 12 months lead with search, Google or AI |
| Polls beat quizzes (the Halloween poll got 18 responses) | A one-click poll in every issue, built as HubSpot link buttons (no Typeform) |
| Testimonials and BTS videos were the only DR content clicked reliably | A short client proof block every month and a small Behind the Screens moment every month |
| Trending subject lines raised opens and lowered clicks | Subject lines are benefit-led. Trending hooks only in the preheader. |
| 5:30pm beat 10am for General in June 2025 | Send at 5:30pm ET; Q2 test confirms or retires it |
| Several tests changed list, time, layout and subject at once | One test per quarter (section 5) |
| 4–6 CTAs per email in the Cooling Drip audit; free analysis offer never stuck | One primary CTA per month, written in this plan before any copy |
| Ownership changed four times; most months late with typo rounds | One owner, this calendar, one Figma template, one HubSpot template, a QA checklist |
| About nine tools in the chain | Three: **Figma** (design), **HubSpot** (send, polls, reports), **this repo** (copy and plan) |
| General and Franchise were built as two separate emails | One build per month. The Franchise version swaps only the proof block, one tip and the subject line. |

## 3. The 2027 format (every month)

Shorter than 2024–25. Readable in about 60 seconds.

1. **Masthead** (image): "Above the Scroll" + month.
2. **H1 + intro** (live text): one main story, 2 short paragraphs.
3. **The 60-Second Version** (live text): 3 tips, subhead + 2 lines each.
4. **Proof** (image + one line of live text): one client result or testimonial.
5. **Quick Poll** (live buttons): one question, 3–4 one-click answers. Every answer is a tracked click.
6. **Behind the Screens** (image + one line): a team member, a moment, a playlist pick.
7. **CTA band** (live text + button): the month's one CTA.
8. **Sign-off + HubSpot footer** with the preference center link.

**Send target:** the last Tuesday of each month at 5:30pm ET, except November (moved before Thanksgiving) and December (moved before the holiday week). Copy locks 10 business days before send; design locks 5 business days before.

**Audience:** contacts who opened or clicked in the last 6 months, plus everyone who clicked in January (see the January re-engagement plan). DR employees go on a suppression list so they stop inflating opens; they get a seed copy instead.

## 4. Month-by-month calendar

Each month has the six fields requested, plus the **H1** (the headline the body is built around), a **Franchise swap** line and the send date. Subject lines are 6–9 words with at most one emoji. Q1 months carry an A (benefit-led) and B (curiosity) line for the Q1 test; other months carry one line, and a second can be written if a test needs it. Preheaders add a second hook rather than repeating the subject. Button colour follows the brand rule: green for consult or contact, orange for downloads and media, blue for read or learn.

## 5. One test per quarter

Only one variable changes per quarter. Everything else stays fixed (list rules, 5:30pm send, layout).

| Quarter | Test | How | Decide by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Jan–Mar) | Subject line style: benefit-led vs. curiosity | HubSpot A/B, 50/50, winner on **click rate**, not opens | Click rate across 3 sends |
| Q2 (Apr–Jun) | Send time: 5:30pm vs. 10am ET (Tuesday) | Split the list randomly in HubSpot | Click rate across 3 sends |
| Q3 (Jul–Sep) | CTA placement: closing band only vs. after the intro and in the closing band | Two versions of the same email | Click rate on the CTA link |
| Q4 (Oct–Dec) | Poll position: after the intro vs. after the tips | Two versions | Poll clicks and total clicks |

## 6. Before January: setup checklist (Nov–Dec 2026)

1. **One owner** named for 2027, with one backup.
2. **List clean-up:** remove hard bounces and role addresses; suppress DR employees and Kickhouse; build the "engaged 6 months" active list. Contacts with no opens since early 2025 get January only; if they don't open Jan or Feb, sunset them.
3. **Fix Salesforce → HubSpot sync** for the fields used in segments (industry, franchise flag, client status) before using them to split the list.
4. **Turn on the HubSpot subscription preferences page** (asked for in April 2024, never built).
5. **Build the HubSpot template once** from the January design (see the build process doc) and save it as "ATS 2027 Master".
6. **Create the poll lists** in HubSpot: one active list per poll answer link (see the January copy doc).
7. **Security:** change the Spotify, Typeform, Airtable and quiz dashboard passwords that appear in Basecamp comments, move them to the password manager, and delete the comments.
8. **Reporting:** one standard HubSpot report (section 7) saved as a dashboard so reports take minutes, not 2–6 hours.

## 7. The monthly report (one screen)

Pulled from HubSpot plus GA4, filled in 7 days after send:

- Delivered, open rate, **click rate**, unsubscribe rate
- Clicks on the main CTA, poll clicks by answer
- Consults booked from UTM `utm_campaign=ats-2027-MM`
- GA4: engaged sessions and average engagement time for `utm_source=hubspot&utm_medium=email`
- One line: what we'll keep, what we'll change next month
